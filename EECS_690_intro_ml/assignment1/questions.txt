a. Why is the value of the accuracy different than the one given for NB in Step 5.4?
There are a couple of reasons. First step 5.4 does stratified sampling for better class balance, and making the training dataset different than ours. Also, it uses cross_val_score which evaluates performance on training set.

b. Why is the confusion matrix different than the one given in Step 6.2?
It's because, first the confusion matrix in step 6.2 is based on a train-test split of 0.8-0.2, comparing to our 0.5-0.5. Also, the predictions made in step 6.2 are based on the SVC model, in comparison to our Gaussian NB model.

c. Why are the P, R, and F1 scores different than those given in Step 6.2?
Because the models are different(SVM classifier vs Gaussian NB), and train-test splits are different(0.8-0.2 vs 0.5-0.5).
